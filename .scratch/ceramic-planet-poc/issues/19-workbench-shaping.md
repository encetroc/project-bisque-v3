# 19 — Craft greenware at the workbench

Status: ready-for-agent
Blocked by: 14, 18

## Scope
Add workbench interaction and a simple selection flow to spend required clay and produce cup/bowl greenware. Vase remains locked until the progression ticket. No minigame.

## Acceptance criteria
- Player can select an available form and clay, optionally glaze, and craft if resources/capacity allow.
- Costs are consumed only on successful output.
- Level 1 exposes cup and bowl.

## Tests
- Recipe tests for successful craft, missing inputs, full inventory, and level-locked vase.
